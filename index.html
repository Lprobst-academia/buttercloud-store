About Buttercloud

Life's busy.
Keep a little joy close.

Buttercloud started with a simple idea: the internet loves a block of butter that you can squish. So we made one that sits on your desk instead of in the fridge.

We keep things small and simple: a few sizes, honest descriptions and no complicated rules. Just something soft to squeeze between emails.
